# Arcadia assets
Put this folder's contents in the Vite static folder (usually public/assets/arcadia/).
brand/: logo-mark.svg, favicon.svg, logo-mark-maskable.svg, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, icon-maskable-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180), favicon-48.png, share-image-1200x630.png.
  The wordmark "Arcadia" is NOT an image: render it as text in the Inter font next to logo-mark.svg.
covers/: 5 profile cover banners (1600x400 SVG, use object-fit: cover). Names: nebula, sunset, ocean, pitch, dusk.
backgrounds/auth-bg.svg: sign-in and onboarding background (dark; keep the form card light or glass).
empty-states/: empty-posts, empty-friends, empty-games, empty-notifications (320x240 SVG, work on light and dark).
game-tiles/: tile-1..6.svg gradient tiles. Put the game's initials on top as text.
Everything here was generated for Arcadia and is free to use. Photos and game art are NOT included.
